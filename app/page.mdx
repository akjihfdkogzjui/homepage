Meng Zhang is an Assistant Professor at the ZJU-UIUC Institute, Zhejiang University.

His research interests include wireless and computer networks, optimization for intelligent networks, edge intelligence, and decentralized machine learning.

## Biography

Meng Zhang received his Ph.D. from the Chinese University of Hong Kong in 2019 and his B.Eng. from South China University of Technology. He conducted postdoctoral research at Northwestern University and was a visiting student research collaborator at Princeton University during his doctoral studies.

## Courses

Meng Zhang teaches courses related to wireless and computer networks, intelligent network optimization, and edge intelligence at the ZJU-UIUC Institute.

## Nexus Lab

Meng Zhang leads the **[Nexus Lab](/nexus)** at Zhejiang University, focusing on research in network economics, age of information, mechanism design, and related areas.
