My group develops the theory and systems for **timely, economically sound, and privacy-preserving** intelligent networks — spanning the age of information, network economics, and trustworthy learning at the network edge. Representative work is grouped into three themes below.

## Timeliness & Edge Intelligence

Delivering *fresh* information and *low-latency* intelligence at the network edge: age-of-information-optimal scheduling, mobile-edge computing, and collaborative inference across edge large language models.
