# Conditional Age-at-Risk for Task Assignment across Heterogeneous Servers

We are glad to share that our paper **"Conditional Age-at-Risk for Task Assignment across Heterogeneous Servers"** has been accepted to the **IEEE International Conference on Computer Communications (INFOCOM 2026)**.

The paper proposes a conditional age-at-risk criterion for assigning tasks across heterogeneous servers, balancing timeliness against tail risk when server speeds and reliability differ.
