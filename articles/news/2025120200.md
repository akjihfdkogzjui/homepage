# Heterogeneous Mean-Field Reinforcement Learning for Age-Minimal GPU Batching

We are glad to share that our paper **"Heterogeneous Mean-Field Reinforcement Learning for Age-Minimal GPU Batching"** has been accepted to the **IEEE International Conference on Computer Communications (INFOCOM 2026)**.

The paper introduces a heterogeneous mean-field reinforcement learning approach to GPU request batching that minimizes the age of information, scaling to many heterogeneous jobs that must be batched for efficient inference while keeping results fresh.
