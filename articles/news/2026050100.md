# When Sample Selection Bias Precipitates Model Collapse

We are glad to share that our paper **"When Sample Selection Bias Precipitates Model Collapse"** has been accepted to the **International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML 2026)**.

The paper analyzes how sample selection bias can precipitate model collapse — the progressive quality loss that can occur when models are trained on selectively filtered or model-generated data — identifying the conditions under which collapse emerges and how it can be mitigated.
